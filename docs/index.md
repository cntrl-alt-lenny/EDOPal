## About EDOPal

Your unofficial companion for EDOPro.

EDOPal is an independent fan-made companion for EDOPro, a Project Ignis project. EDOPal has no affiliation with or endorsement from Project Ignis, Konami or Shueisha. Yu-Gi-Oh! and related marks belong to their respective rights holders.

Card artwork is served by YGOProDeck and Project Ignis. Tournament results and deck lists come from Format Library. Game-cover sources, including LaunchBox Games Database, are recorded in the music catalogue. Thonk uses Kenney’s CC0 audio. Inter is distributed under the SIL Open Font License.
